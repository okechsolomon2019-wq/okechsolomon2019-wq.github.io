Privacy notice.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Current website behavior: account details and booking requests are saved only in your browser on your device. They are not sent to Moses Lounge, and requests are not confirmed automatically. Contact the manager to confirm a booking.

Information stored on your device

If you create an account, the website stores your email address, name, and a salted password hash in your browser storage. If you make a booking request, it stores the selected service, unit if applicable, date, party size, notes, and price information there. The password itself is not saved as plain text.

This front-end demonstration does not send these account or booking details to the manager or a booking server. The information does not automatically sync to another browser or device. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to access its saved website data. Do not use an important password for this demonstration.

Storage, deletion, and choices

Saved accounts and booking requests remain in the browser's local storage until you clear the website's stored data or remove the browser profile. Logging out ends the saved local sign-in session but does not erase saved account or booking records. There is currently no server-side account deletion or booking-submission function.

Google sign-in

The Google sign-in button is not connected. Entering a Gmail address in the regular email form is not Google authentication or Google verification.

Offline files and external fonts

After a successful visit, the website may cache its static pages and assets on your device to support offline viewing. The site requests typefaces from Google Fonts; your browser may contact Google's font service to retrieve them, subject to Google's own privacy terms.

Contact and booking confirmation

For booking availability, corrections, or questions, contact manager Mandel Peter at +211 929 994 005 or +211 923 759 776. Please do not send passwords or payment card details by phone.

This notice describes the current demo website behavior and is not a substitute for legal advice. Before publishing an app or collecting information through a live booking service, Moses Lounge should review and update this notice to reflect the actual service, retention, security, contact details, and applicable privacy laws.
